# Netflix Data Analysis Project – Content Trends & Strategic Insights

## Project Overview
Netflix is one of the world’s leading streaming platforms, continuously expanding its content library across movies and TV shows. With rising competition from platforms like Amazon Prime, Disney+, and regional OTT services, it is essential to analyze content trends to support strategic decision-making.

This project focuses on analyzing Netflix’s content catalog to identify trends in content type, genres, countries, ratings, and duration over time, and to provide strategic recommendations based on data-driven insights.

---

## Problem Statement
To analyze Netflix’s content distribution and growth patterns in order to understand:
- The shift between Movies and TV Shows
- Popular genres and audience preferences
- Country-wise content contribution
- Content ratings and duration trends
- Netflix’s evolution into a global content platform

---

## Objectives
- Analyze the growth of Movies vs TV Shows over time  
- Identify dominant genres and emerging content trends  
- Study country-wise content distribution  
- Examine ratings and duration patterns  
- Generate strategic insights for content planning and expansion  

---

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains information related to:
- Content type (Movie / TV Show)
- Genre
- Country
- Release year
- Ratings
- Duration

2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
3. Visualization of content trends using:
   - Bar charts
   - Line plots
   - Heatmaps  
4. Interpretation of results and insight generation  

---

## Key Insights
- Significant growth in TV Shows compared to Movies  
- Dominance of genres such as Drama, Comedy, and Documentary  
- Rapid rise in international content, especially from India, UK, and South Korea  
- Diverse ratings indicating varied audience targeting  
- Netflix’s transformation from a movie-focused platform to a global content powerhouse
